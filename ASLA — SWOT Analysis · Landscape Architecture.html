Where SWOT Becomes Strategy
How to use this section

The real power of a SWOT isn't in the four quadrants — it's in the intersections. Look diagonally and ask the strategic questions below. Strategy built without this step is just wishful thinking dressed up as a plan.

SO
Strengths × Opportunities
Where should you double down and invest?
"We have [strength] and the market needs [opportunity] — this is where we invest aggressively."
Your S×O Strategy
WT
Weaknesses × Threats
Where are you most exposed — and what must be addressed first?
"Our [weakness] combined with [threat] is our most urgent strategic risk. Here's what we're doing about it."
Your W×T Risk Response
ST
Strengths × Threats
How do your strengths protect you from headwinds?
"Even if [threat] materializes, our [strength] insulates us because..."
Your S×T Defense
