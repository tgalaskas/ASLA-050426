Strategic Insights
Existing clients (35%) are your highest-value source. Every project close should include a 6-month follow-up touchpoint.
Referrals convert at the second-highest rate. Build a formal ask-at-project-close habit and maintain your referral network proactively.
Industry colleagues (15%) are high-value but fragile. These relationships require consistent nurturing at events, lunches, and ASLA chapter involvement.
RFQ/RFP (15%) is reactive and competitive. The goal is to be known before the RFP drops — not to discover an opportunity when it posts.
LinkedIn and Events are emerging channels. Consistent visibility at 4+ events per year and regular LinkedIn activity compounds over time.
