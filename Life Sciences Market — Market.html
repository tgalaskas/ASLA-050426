⬇ 2026 — Headwind Year
Contraction
NIH grant freezes stall early-stage biotech. National lab vacancy at 21.4%. ARE Boston fell to 86.4% occupancy. Developers pausing speculative starts. BUT San Diego at 97.2% is expanding. Campus Point BMS groundbreaking 2026. Most competitors go quiet. Maximum relationship-building window — use it.
→ 2027 — Transition Year
Stabilization
Vacancy absorbs nationally. Pipeline deliveries taper — few projects started in 2025–26 deliver in 2027. VC funding steady. Pharma M&A picks up ("M&A is the lifeblood of this industry" — Hallie Kuhn, ARE). Teams positioned in 2026 start receiving calls as developers re-engage architects for 2028 pipeline. Pre-construction typically starts 18–24 months before groundbreaking.
⬆ 2028 — Uptick Year
Recovery
Campus Point BMS delivers (466K RSF). ARE's 19.9M RSF future pipeline activates. New ground-up starts accelerate in strongest markets. Firms with ARE relationships built in 2026 win this work. Firms that waited in 2026 start from zero against firms who've been showing up for two years. The gap is real and it compounds.

The National Headwinds — Why Most Competitors Are Quiet
  • 1
    NIH Funding Freeze
    • Proposed cuts stall early-stage academic and government-backed research
    • NIH funding → biotech hiring → lab space demand
    • ARE's government tenant base (NIH/GSA) under direct pressure
  • 2
    Tariff Uncertainty
    • Steel, aluminum, manufactured components — costs unpredictable
    • Developers delaying groundbreaking until cost models stabilize
    • Extends pre-construction phase — when LAs should be engaged
  • 3
    Elevated Interest Rates
    • Higher cost of capital makes development math harder
    • ARE reducing non-income-producing assets from 20% → 11–16%
    • Selective, funded projects continue — speculative starts pause
  • 4
    Lab Space Glut — National
    • 21.4% vacancy nationally (CBRE Q1 2026)
    • Boston most acute — ARE occupancy dropped to 86.4%
    • San Diego exception — 97.2% occupancy, developers still building
  • 5
    Global Uncertainty
    • FDA leadership changes, drug pricing pressure, geopolitical risk
    • "Lingering conservatism" dampens leasing velocity nationally
    • San Diego's supply-demand balance insulates the local market

Why San Diego Is Different — And Why Now Is Right

The firms that show up now are the ones who get called first when the market turns. This happened after 2008. It happened after COVID. It will happen again in 2028.
