Procurement Sequence — Which Scenario Are You In?

Critical Reality — LA Firms Are Almost Always in the Second WaveThe prime architect (Gensler SD, HDR) is selected by ARE first. The landscape architect is assembled by the prime — not directly hired by ARE — for most new campus projects. Know which scenario you're in before investing pursuit resources. The existing campus refresh scopes (Torrey Pines, Lilly Gateway Labs area) may be the exception where ARE engages an LA directly.
A
Long Lead — Prime Not Yet Engaged
Best position — you're in this right now. The prime architect for Campus Point BMS is not publicly confirmed. Build relationships with Gensler SD and HDR now — before they're in pursuit mode. When the prime is selected and starts assembling their sub team, you want to be their first call for landscape. Use 2026 to establish these relationships while calendars have room.
B
Prime Shortlisted or Selected
React quickly. When ARE announces a prime architect appointment for Campus Point, congratulate them — this is a legitimate, warm reason to reach out and open the conversation. Conduct your go/no-go for each shortlisted prime. Which team is most likely to win, and where do you add the most value to their team?
C
Existing Campus Refresh — Best First Project
Best entry point for a first ARE project. The existing 1.3M RSF at Campus Point and re-leasing activity at Torrey Pines (Lilly Gateway Labs already delivered with "event lawn" and "walking path") generate smaller refresh scopes. Lower barrier, may engage LA directly with ARE's development team. A smaller project win is the credential that opens the door to the BMS build-to-suit.

BD Opportunity — What the Risks Mean for Landscape Architecture

Where the Work Is
· Campus refresh & re-leasing — Vacant spaces get exterior upgrades to attract tenants. $71M in 2026 lease expirations + $72M in 2027 = steady refresh pipeline. Entry plazas, outdoor amenities, and campus environments are active spending categories.
· Land activation (direct hire) — ARE recognized $1.45B in impairments, 50–60% land-related. Impaired land still needs to be activated to preserve value — direct landscape contracts for park-first development and amenity refreshes that don't flow through an architect.
· Mission-critical ground-up — BMS Campus Point (426,927 RSF) and Novartis build-to-suit (466,598 RSF) are both proceeding in San Diego. Landscape coordination is active now. The design teams are already assembled — subconsultant relationships are the entry point.
· ESG & LEED (direct prime) — Site-level metrics (water reduction, carbon sequestration, stormwater) are calculated at the landscape level. ARE often engages LAs directly for district-wide scopes spanning multiple building phases and architects.
· Residential conversion — ARE is selling disposed land to residential developers. Firms with life science and residential campus experience can follow the land — the buyers need landscape services and may arrive through the ARE relationship.
· Risk mitigation positioning — ARE operates in wildfire zones (NorCal), seismic zones (SF, SD, Seattle), and coastal vulnerability areas. Fire-resistant planting, bioretention, and stormwater design position the LA as a risk-reduction partner, not just an aesthetic vendor.
