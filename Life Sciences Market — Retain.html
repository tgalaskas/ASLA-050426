Three Things. Every Project. No Exceptions.

1
Request a Debrief
Whether you win or lose, ask for a debrief. ARE's development team will tell you exactly what differentiated the winning firm — and what you need to fix. Most clients are surprised when consultants ask. That surprise is itself a differentiator — it signals you're serious about continuous improvement, not just the next pitch.
2
Ask for a Reference or Introduction
After a successful delivery, ask for a reference letter AND an introduction. "Is there someone at Gensler SD or HDR you'd feel comfortable introducing us to?" The warm intro is worth more than any cold outreach campaign. Ask when the relationship is warm — not six months after the project closes and the relationship has gone dormant.
3
Schedule the Next Touchpoint
Book a 6-month check-in before you leave the final project meeting. ARE's 19.9M RSF future SD pipeline means there will always be a next conversation to have. Don't let the relationship go dormant. The next project begins in this conversation — not in a prospecting call 18 months later.
